Pohádkomat does not tell the story for you. It helps you tell it.

Bedtime, your child wants a story and nothing comes to mind? Pohádkomat hands you the building blocks — characters, a quest for the hero, a problem in the way and an ending — and keeps the outline in front of you. You tell the story to your child in your own words. The app never speaks and reads nothing aloud.

• Spin the globe and tap a country: it decides which tradition tonight's tale comes from.
• 150 folk tales free from fifteen regions of the world, already on your phone.
• Pick one to six characters, a quest, a problem and an ending. They fold into a one-screen "tale in brief".
• The prompter walks you through step by step and, when you get stuck, sends a hint to push off from. Never a sentence to read out.
• An illustration for every step, and sounds that you play yourself: a fox, an owl, a dragon, wind, thunder, a spell.
• The motifs come from real public-domain tales: Erben, Němcová, the Brothers Grimm, Andersen, Perrault and collections from Africa, Asia, the Caucasus and the Americas.
• Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.

The app speaks your language (45 languages). The tales, hints and sound names are in Czech for now.
